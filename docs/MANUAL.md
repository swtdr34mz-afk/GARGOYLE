# GARGOYLE – Manual

GARGOYLE is an 8-bit drone synth for the M-VAVE FM-1, made from the sound chips of the old home computers, consoles
and DOS games (NES, Game Boy, C64, Atari POKEY, AdLib, PC speaker). No reverb: an echo that stores 8 bits.

**Screen:** every page is its own room with its own monster (SUMMON orc, GROOVE skeleton, SETUP treasure, ECHO crystal
ball, RITUAL slime, LORE beholder, SPELL the flooded room); WRATH past half brings the dragon, FREEZE turns everything
ice blue. Right: the dome (algorithm number, GROOVE steps), the page, HOLD, octave, battery (three hearts). The four
knobs are four heroes with fixed colours.

**Pages (one knob, one parameter):** HOME SUMMON: CURSE (left ring, right sync/FM), HORDE (left octaves, right detune),
RUNE (wave, duty, table), ALCHEMY (per algorithm, shown when turned). HOME 2×/3×: GROOVE and SETUP. FX ECHO: ECHO, HAUNT
(feedback, left ping-pong), DECAY (duller, grainier repeats), DEPTH. ENV RITUAL: TORCH (left low pass, right high
pass), PORTAL (a detent a semitone, ±24), AWAKEN, SLUMBER. EDIT LORE: CREEP, WANDER, BITS (16 .. 1), VOLUME. GLO
SPELL (all algorithms): AGE (sample rate down to 2 kHz), AURA (a resonator on root, fifth and octaves: the chip tones ring on into a drone), SPEED (1–60 Hz:
arpeggios, patterns), WIDTH. LFO OMEN I–IV (press LFO again for the next), four LFOs, each on several targets at once (they add up): RATE (free, or note values 4 bars .. 1/32
when the GROOVE runs, also with MIDI clock), SHAPE (sine, triangle, saw, square, stairs, chance, drift, swell, heart, bounce, chaos, turing), POWER, PATH (BREATH,
FLAME, HAUNT, AURA, RUNE, CURSE, DECAY, STORM, MORPH, TORCH; the sound only, no panning).

**Algorithms (PRESET):** 1 TORCHLIGHT, 2 CRYPT CHOIR, 3 GOBLIN CHORDS, 4 DRAGON BREATH, 5 PC SPEAKER, 6 POKEY CURSE,
7 SID RITUAL, 8 ADLIB HALL, 9 BEHOLDER, 10 TREASURE, 11 SKELETON MARCH, 12 MAGE TOWER, 13 THRONE OF BONES, 14 PIT OF
DOOM, 15 WIND OF ABYSS, 16 SPELLBOOK. ALGORITHM: QUEST = MORPH into the next algorithm (it plays with its own knobs; both sound in between). REC: FATE (dice, also the variants: wave, chord, scale; held 1 s deep). SEQ held:
factory sound.

**WRATH (SELECT):** one macro over everything for live play, 4 a detent.

**GROOVE:** HOME twice, REC on/off, ALGORITHM style: HEARTBEAT, FOOTSTEPS, STONES, CHAINS, WINGS, RITUAL, BELL, BONES,
RUMBLE, TORCH – chiptune sound effects (square, 4-bit triangle, noise register, 16 volume steps, 60 frames a second),
tuned to the drone. DENSITY, BREATH, DISTANCE, TEMPO; SETUP: LEVEL, HUMAN, FOLLOW, TUNE. Follows a MIDI clock.

**More:** PLAY HOLD, ARP FREEZE, SAVE, 27 snapshots (SAVE / SEL + any key). MIDI: notes,
pitch bend ±2, CC 1 RUNE, CC 70–73 CURSE HORDE RUNE ALCHEMY, CC 75 WRATH, clock / start / stop.
