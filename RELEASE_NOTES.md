**GARGOYLE v0.1.2**

- new page **OMEN** on the LFO button: a real LFO that moves several knobs at once
  - RATE: free from one wave a minute up to 20 Hz; while the GROOVE runs in note values (4 bars … 1/32), locked to the bar, also with MIDI clock
  - SHAPE: sine, triangle, saw, square, stairs (8 steps like a chip), chance (a random value each wave)
  - POWER: how strong (fully left = off)
  - PATH: where it goes – BREATH, FLAME, HAUNT, AURA, TIDE, CURSE, DECAY, STORM
  - its room: the crystal ball in violet, pulsing with the LFO
- **SPELL** (AGE, AURA, SPEED, WIDTH) moved to the **GLO** button; the level meter is gone

Install: download the .fwsc below and flash it with the M-Flasher. Your sounds and snapshots stay.
